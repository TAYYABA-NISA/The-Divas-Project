Monitoring is optional for the examination workflow. It creates advisory flags for an examiner to review and never changes your score automatically.

What the local agent may send

  • Advisory events such as a prolonged gaze away, no face, multiple faces, or a phone.
  • Short private evidence clips only when an event is flagged.
  • Hashed Bluetooth device identifiers and RSSI readings for an optional baseline.

Lighting, glasses, head coverings, camera position, and noisy Bluetooth signals can produce false flags. Flags are hints for human review, not proof of misconduct.
